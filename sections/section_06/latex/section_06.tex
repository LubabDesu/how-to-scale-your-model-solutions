\textbf{Chapter:} Training LLaMA 3 on TPUs\\

\textbf{Reference:} \url{https://jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book/applied-training/}

\paragraph{Hardware and training assumptions.}
A full TPU v5p pod has
\[
16\times20\times28=8960\ \text{chips}.
\]
Each chip has bfloat16 peak compute
\[
C=4.59\times10^{14}\ \text{FLOPs/s}.
\]
The ICI operational intensity is approximately 2550, and the DCN operational
intensity is
\[
\frac{C}{W_{\mathrm{DCN}}}
=\frac{4.59\times10^{14}}{6.25\times10^9}
\approx73{,}440.
\]
The batch size is $B=2^{22}=4{,}194{,}304$ tokens and MFU is 40\%.

\section*{Question 1: Scaling LLaMA 3-70B to four pods}

Use data parallelism across the four pods and mixed FSDP plus tensor
parallelism within each pod. Each pod receives
\[
B_{\mathrm{pod}}=\frac{4{,}194{,}304}{4}
=1{,}048{,}576\ \text{tokens}.
\]
This is well above the DCN requirement:
\[
1{,}048{,}576>73{,}440,
\]
so inter-pod data-parallel communication can be hidden by compute.

Across all four pods, the local batch is
\[
\frac{B}{4(8960)}
\approx117.0\ \text{tokens per chip}.
\]
For LLaMA 3-70B, $F=28{,}672$, so the mixed FSDP and tensor-parallel
threshold is
\[
\frac{2550^2}{2F}
=\frac{2550^2}{2(28672)}
\approx113.4\ \text{tokens per chip}.
\]
Since $117.0>113.4$, the continuous roofline model says this setup is barely
compute-bound.

Within each pod, the idealized FSDP degree is
\begin{align*}
X_{\mathrm{opt}}
&=\sqrt{\frac{2B_{\mathrm{pod}}N_{\mathrm{pod}}}{F}}\\
&=\sqrt{\frac{2(1{,}048{,}576)(8960)}{28{,}672}}\\
&\approx810,
\end{align*}
with $Y=N_{\mathrm{pod}}/X\approx11$-way tensor parallelism. The exact
integer layout would be rounded to match the physical pod topology.

The one-pod estimate is approximately 44 days at 40\% MFU. Four pods provide
four times the compute, so
\[
\boxed{T_{\mathrm{train}}\approx\frac{44}{4}=11\ \text{days}}.
\]

\section*{Question 2: LLaMA 3-405B}

\subsection*{(a) Parameters and training FLOPs}

The model configuration is
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
\toprule
Hyperparameter & Value \\
\midrule
Layers $L$ & 126 \\
Model dimension $D$ & 16,384 \\
Feed-forward dimension $F$ & 53,248 \\
Query heads $N$ & 128 \\
KV heads $K$ & 8 \\
Head dimension $H$ & 128 \\
Vocabulary size $V$ & 128,256 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The gated MLP contributes
\[
P_{\mathrm{MLP}}=3LDF
=329{,}772{,}957{,}696.
\]
Grouped-query attention contributes
\begin{align*}
P_{\mathrm{attention}}
&=2LDH(N+K)\\
&=71{,}873{,}593{,}344.
\end{align*}
The separate input and output embeddings contribute
\[
P_{\mathrm{vocab}}=2VD
=4{,}202{,}692{,}608.
\]
Therefore,
\begin{align*}
P
&=P_{\mathrm{MLP}}+P_{\mathrm{attention}}+P_{\mathrm{vocab}}\\
&=405{,}849{,}243{,}648\\
&\approx\boxed{405.85\text{B parameters}}.
\end{align*}

Training takes approximately six FLOPs per parameter per token:
\[
\boxed{\text{FLOPs/token}\approx6P
=2.435\times10^{12}}.
\]
For 15 trillion tokens,
\begin{align*}
F_{\mathrm{total}}
&=6P(15\times10^{12})\\
&=3.653\times10^{25}\ \text{FLOPs}.
\end{align*}

\subsection*{(b) Training on eight pods}

Use data parallelism across the eight pods, with mixed FSDP and tensor
parallelism inside each pod. Each pod receives
\[
B_{\mathrm{pod}}=\frac{4{,}194{,}304}{8}
=524{,}288\ \text{tokens}.
\]
Since $524{,}288>73{,}440$, the inter-pod DCN AllReduce is below its roofline
bound.

The local batch across all $8(8960)=71{,}680$ chips is
\[
\frac{B}{71{,}680}
\approx58.5\ \text{tokens per chip}.
\]
For $F=53{,}248$, the mixed-parallel compute-bound threshold is
\[
\frac{2550^2}{2F}
=\frac{2550^2}{2(53248)}
\approx61.1\ \text{tokens per chip}.
\]
Thus the configuration is slightly communication-bound within each pod.
Pure FSDP would be much further below its approximately 850-token local
batch requirement.

The continuous optimum inside one pod is
\[
X_{\mathrm{opt}}
=\sqrt{\frac{2B_{\mathrm{pod}}N_{\mathrm{pod}}}{F}}
\approx420,
\qquad
Y_{\mathrm{opt}}\approx21.
\]
A nearby topology-aligned split is $X=448$ FSDP shards and $Y=20$ tensor
shards.

Ignoring communication gives the compute lower bound
\begin{align*}
T_{\mathrm{compute}}
&=\frac{3.653\times10^{25}}
{8(8960)(0.4)(4.59\times10^{14})}\\
&=2.775\times10^6\ \text{s}\\
&\approx32.1\ \text{days}.
\end{align*}
Because the ideal mixed layout is only slightly communication-bound, the
roofline estimate rises to approximately
\[
\boxed{32\text{--}33\ \text{days}}.
\]
