\section*{Question 1: Reading the suspicious profile}

The profile contains a reduction, two large fusions containing matrix
multiplications, and a final AllReduce.  The underlying computation is
\[
\operatorname{out}
=\operatorname{einsum}(\texttt{wf,bf->bw},W_2,
  \operatorname{einsum}(\texttt{fw,bw->bf},W_1,x)).
\]
Equivalently, this is the two-layer linear map
\[
h=xW_1^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
\operatorname{out}=hW_2^{\mathsf T}.
\]

\subsection*{First fusion}

The HLO shows the local computation
\[
\mathrm{bf16}[8192]\times
\mathrm{bf16}[4096,8192]
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{bf16}[4096],
\]
contracting over the width dimension \(8192\).  The final AllReduce has
replica groups of size eight, revealing eight-way model parallelism.
Therefore the feed-forward dimension is sharded eight ways:
\[
F_{\mathrm{local}}=4096,
\qquad
F=8(4096)=32768.
\]
Restoring the batch dimension, the true first matmul is
\[
\boxed{
x[8,8192]\,W_1^{\mathsf T}[8192,32768]
\longrightarrow h[8,32768]}.
\]
Every model-parallel device computes \(4096\) columns of \(h\).

\subsection*{Second fusion and AllReduce}

For one batch element, each device multiplies its local hidden vector by
the corresponding slice of \(W_2\):
\[
h_y[4096]\,W_{2,y}^{\mathsf T}[4096,8192]
\longrightarrow p_y[8192].
\]
Restoring the batch dimension, the same local computation is
\[
h_y[8,4096]\,W_{2,y}^{\mathsf T}[4096,8192]
\longrightarrow p_y[8,8192].
\]
These are partial sums over disjoint pieces of the contracted feed-forward
dimension.  The AllReduce adds them:
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{out}
=\sum_{y=0}^{7}p_y
\in\mathbb{R}^{8\times8192}}.
\]
Thus the AllReduce is required because the sharded axis is a contracting
axis in the second matmul; it is not an AllGather.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Profile operation & Local meaning & Global meaning \\
\midrule
Reduction & preprocessing feeding \(x\) & not the model-parallel combine \\
First fusion & \(8192\to4096\) matmul & \(8192\to32768\) up-projection \\
Second fusion & \(4096\to8192\) matmul & partial down-projection \\
AllReduce & sum eight partial outputs & final \([8,8192]\) result \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The suspicious-looking small \(4096\) dimension is only the per-device
view.  The true feed-forward width is \(32768\), split over eight devices,
and the output is reconstructed by summing the eight partial
down-projections.
